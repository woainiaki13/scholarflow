New user tutorial

FINISH ONE REAL TASK

Understand one idea from your lecture.

You do not need to learn eight tools first. Start with one question, write your own explanation, check the original and keep what changed.

01 / UPLOAD ONCE

Let the system organise the file.

You provide a text PDF, PPTX, TXT or Markdown. ScholarFlow saves the original, reads and splits the text, then prepares notes, evidence cards and known reference information.

02 / CHOOSE ONE IDEA

Find something worth explaining.

Open the learning pack and choose an idea you want to understand. The revision guide offers question starters from your actual notes; you can edit the question.

03 / EXPLAIN FIRST

Write what you think, and why.

Your input is a question and your own explanation. Choose Get feedback on my explanation to get source-grounded feedback. The guide does not write the answer or run AI for you.

04 / CHECK THE SOURCE

Inspect the passage behind the feedback.

Your output keeps your explanation beside feedback and available source quotations. Open a quotation or the original text; check wording, conditions and missing context yourself.

05 / KEEP THE CHANGE

Save your revised understanding.

Below completed feedback, write what you would explain differently and choose Save reflection to group. Your original explanation, feedback and revision stay together.

06 / RETURN TOMORROW

Record what actually happened.

Keep your task, time spent, corrections and difficulties in a study-session record. Your own observations help you decide whether this workflow is useful over several days.

AI feedback is something to inspect. An exact quotation match confirms wording in the supplied text, not the correctness of every interpretation. Unsubmitted explanations and session drafts stay on your device; submitted inputs and results are shared with your group.
How do I begin with my group?

Open the same full invitation link as your teammate, then set your display name in Share group. Anyone with that link can read and contribute. Wait for Cloud synced after completing a task.

How do I check a different definition after the first one?

In Check my understanding, find Check another concept. Type a new concept, such as Investment banking, and choose Start this concept. Write its definition in My explanation, then run the check. The earlier concept and feedback remain in history. You can also choose a saved concept from the dropdown. Both checks use the selected course material.

How do From and To work in Connect ideas?

Saved concepts from the current learning pack load automatically. From is the starting idea; To is the related idea. Describe how or when they relate, choose Add this connection, and repeat for other pairs. Select Check my connections for source-grounded feedback. If no saved concepts are available, choose Generate new concepts from source first. Some materials may not contain enough related concepts.

My groupmate cannot see my material. What should I check?

Compare your complete invitation links, including the room parameter. Both pages must use the same link. Click Sync now and wait for Cloud synced. A different group link opens a different library.

What if an upload fails or the page is refreshed?

Check the failed item in Automation inbox. If an original-file link is available, the upload has been retained. Interrupted tasks are not automatically charged again. Check the Dify run log before retrying the file.

Does this share my whole Google Drive?

No. The owner's Google Drive archive runs separately every five minutes. It does not automatically import into this group. Add a file through this workspace to share it here.

Can I keep a copy or bring in older work?

Export workspace backup saves a JSON copy. Share group includes an option to import previous materials and practice from this browser, skipping built-in DEMO sources. The imported records become visible to your group.
